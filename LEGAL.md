## Maryland Legal Framework

### Surplus Recovery Is Legal Without a Law License in Maryland

Surplus fund finders/recovery agents operate legally in Maryland under contract law principles. A finder who:

1. Identifies surplus funds held in the court registry
2. Locates the rightful claimant (former homeowner)
3. Enters a **written contingency fee agreement** before providing any services
4. Assists the claimant in filing a Petition for Release of Surplus Funds

...is not practicing law. The agreement is between a private party (finder) and the claimant. The finder does not appear in court and does not give legal advice.

**Key requirement:** The written agreement must be signed **before any assistance is provided** — including before discussing the specific case details with the claimant.

### What ClearPath MUST Do Before Outreach

1. **Have a signed contingency fee agreement template** reviewed by Maryland counsel
2. **Disclose in every outreach communication:**
   - ClearPath is not a law firm or attorney
   - ClearPath is not affiliated with the court
   - The claimant is not required to use a finder — they may file their own claim
   - The specific fee percentage being offered
3. **Include in the agreement:**
   - Case number and court where funds are held
   - Specific surplus amount (if known) or estimated range
   - Exact fee percentage
   - Claimant's right to verify funds independently via the court
   - Claimant's right to cancel within 3 business days (recommended)

---

### What ClearPath CANNOT Do

- Cannot represent the claimant in court proceedings
- Cannot negotiate on the claimant's behalf with lienholders
- Cannot advise on tax implications of recovery
- Cannot file the Petition themselves — claimant or their attorney files
- Cannot contact a claimant who has hired an attorney without attorney's consent (Md. Rules of Professional Conduct Rule 4.2 — though this rule technically applies to lawyers, following it is good practice)

## Recommended Contingency Fee Structure

| Surplus Amount | Recommended Fee | Justification |
|---------------|-----------------|---------------|
| $5,000 – $15,000 | 40% | Small cases; high per-case labor cost relative to recovery |
| $15,001 – $50,000 | 35% | Mid-range; still significant finding/filing work |
| $50,001 – $100,000 | 30% | Large cases; owner more likely to negotiate or hire own attorney |
| $100,000+ | 25% or negotiate | At this scale, claimant will likely want independent legal counsel |

**Do not exceed 40%.** While Maryland has no statutory cap, agreements above 40% have been challenged as unconscionable in other states' analogous cases. 40% is the industry norm and is defensible.

**Always offer the claimant the option to negotiate** — starting at 40% and settling at 35% after negotiation creates goodwill and improves agreement rates.
